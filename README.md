# 1st floor walkthrough

Open it here: **https://sherlyguides.github.io/1st-floor-walkthrough/**

Walk through the 1st floor in any browser, on a laptop or a phone. Nothing to install. A short tour on screen shows the controls the first time.

- **Laptop:** click once and the mouse aims your view; click again to get your cursor back. Walk with W A S D or scroll the mouse wheel. Click a door to open it, or press F while aiming.
- **Phone:** drag to look, use the circle to walk, tap a door to open it.
- **Orbit, Walk, Fly** at the bottom switch between looking at the whole floor and walking through it.

Made with [walkable](https://sherlyguides.github.io/walkables/).
